Rules that keep it honest
1
The rail lives in the shell, above the router. Changing step swaps only the panel below it — no reload, no flicker, no re-entry animation.
2
The underline is real progress: weighted by sections confirmed, never by time elapsed or a fake creep to 90%.
3
It never runs backwards. Regenerating one section in step 4 does not drop the rail back to step 3.
4
A completed stop is a link. Consultants revisit scope constantly and should not have to restart to change a package name.
5
Sub-labels carry the count that matters at that moment — "5 of 12 confirmed" — so the second progress layer is readable from the rail alone.
6
State is server-side on the session. Closing the tab and coming back tomorrow restores the same stop, mid-review.
